# 📇 Contacts

How to reach Psanctuary, the church, from its decentralized, playful side on GitHub. Every address
and channel here is one the church publishes on [psanctuary.org](https://www.psanctuary.org) for
this purpose; nobody's personal details are listed. The same paths open as one window from
**Reach out** on every Psanctuary page on GitHub.

*Last checked 2026-10-22.*

## ✉️ Write to the church

| Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [info@psanctuary.org](mailto:info@psanctuary.org) | The church's public address: membership, Sunday service, ceremonies, courses, giving (and receipts for gifts), or these pages |
| [The contact form](https://www.psanctuary.org/contact) | In the church's own words: "To reach our team, please fill out the form below or e-mail info@psanctuary.org." |

## 🍄 Where the community gathers

| Where | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Psanctuary Member Community](https://psanctuary-member-community.mn.co/) | The church's member network on Mighty Networks: events, member-led clubs, courses and messages, on the web and in the Mighty app. One link: visitors see how to join, and members land on their dashboard |

**Why there's no Discord.** Psanctuary once had a Discord; Eric O closed it on purpose, so the
community gathers in one place, on Mighty Networks. A Discord may only ever come back as a help-desk
back end most people would never see, unless a DAO or something new grows here. Time will tell.

Something broken or confusing on Psanctuary's GitHub pages? Write to
